Current state: **`FEASIBLE_FOR_BOUNDED_PROFILING_ONLY`**.

Allowed next work:

- design bounded schema/profile experiments for the three v1 sources;
- strengthen license/privacy evidence;
- verify a finite history protocol; and
- verify source identifier, status, date, and coordinate fields.

- full-history crawling;
- declaring a source primary key;
- creating closure/survival labels;
- committing real data to Git;
- publishing on Kaggle; or
- implementing the other 192 current categories.

1. finite history lower bound, retention, completeness, and termination rule;
2. Kaggle raw-record redistribution rights;
3. public privacy allowlist;
4. establishment/permit identity semantics;
5. closure/lifecycle semantics; and
6. actual X/Y source-field meaning and axis order.
